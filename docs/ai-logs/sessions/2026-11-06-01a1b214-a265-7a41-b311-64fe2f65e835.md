## 2026-11-06T23:58:53+05:30 — AI response

```text
**Part 3 turns the rebalance recommendation into a token transfer, then proves the tokens reached the destination lending pool.**

For example, if the decision engine recommends moving your test tokens from the Sepolia pool to the Base Sepolia pool, Part 3 handles the execution:

1. **Check the decision again.** Re-read balances, rates and fees. Cancel the move if it no longer meets your approved policy.
2. **Execute the approved move.** Use CRE to deliver the instruction to the wallet contract. The contract withdraws the tokens and sends them through Chainlink CCIP.
3. **Track the transfer.** Record the operation, source transaction and CCIP message so tracking survives a process restart.
4. **Verify the deposit.** Confirm the correct token and amount arrived at the intended wallet and were deposited into its lending pool.
5. **Recover safely.** If delivery or the deposit fails, recover the existing operation without accidentally sending the principal twice.

The key rule is: **“the source transaction succeeded” does not mean “the rebalance is complete.”** Completion requires evidence of the destination lending position.

This part stays on the two approved testnets. The mock pools do not earn real interest. WhatsApp integration belongs to Part 4.
```
